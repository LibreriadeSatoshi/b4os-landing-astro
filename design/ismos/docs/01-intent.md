## Two ISMOS, one system

There are two ISMOS. The Foundation is the governance layer: a board, grants, reports, fellowships, and the people who fund the work. The school is what students and teachers meet: the platform at ISMOS.XYZ, the live cohorts and open courses, the teachers, and the builders who come out the other side. Both carry the same mark. They do not sound the same and they do not use colour the same way.

| | Foundation | ISMOS (the school) |
|---|---|---|
| The mark | Identical | Identical |
| Descender line | ISMOS.FOUNDATION | ISMOS.XYZ |
| Neutrals | Ink, Paper | Ink, Paper, unchanged |
| Colour logic | One structural accent | The ISMOS palette |
| Title face | Spectral | Literata, for all titles |
| Wordmark face | Spectral | Bricolage Grotesque, more welcoming than the title face and used nowhere else |
| Text and UI face | IBM Plex Sans | IBM Plex Sans, unchanged |
| Register | Institutional | Warm, restrained |

**Naming.** Write ISMOS on platform surfaces. Write ISMOS.XYZ when the sentence needs the address. Write ISMOS Foundation in full, on first reference, when the Foundation is genuinely the actor: a grant, a board decision, a fellowship. No *ISMOS School*, *ISMOS Academy*, *ISMOS Platform*. No logo, monogram or wordmark for a tag: a tag is a word, never a second mark and never a colour.

## Who this is for

Three readers, in this order. Most of the people making ISMOS materials do not work for ISMOS, and some of them are not people.

1. **ISMOS staff.** Follow the fixed values exactly: colour, type, grid, minimum sizes. Where the system gives a rule, the rule is the answer. Where it does not, decide, then write the decision into the next version.
2. **Teachers and ambassadors.** You are making slides, handouts and posts for your own cohort, often on your own laptop, sometimes offline. Get four things right: the palette, the mark used whole, Literata or Plex from the shared files, and the address written as ISMOS.XYZ. If everything else drifts, those four still hold it together.
3. **AI tools generating assets.** Values here are literal instructions. Hex values are exact. Word limits are counted, not estimated. Artwork is placed, never redrawn. Where a section says never, there is no permitted exception. Section 08 is the entry point.

- **Fixed:** the mark and its geometry, the palette and its hex values, Ink and Paper, the three typefaces plus Bricolage Grotesque for the wordmark, minimum sizes, contrast minimums.
- **Yours to judge:** composition, how much white space, how long the copy runs, whether a page needs an image at all.
- **If in doubt:** make the plainer version. A student meeting this material for the first time should be able to read the words straight away, without stopping to decode a diagram, a colour or a layout first.

## Components

CSS components in `components/bundle.css`, each with a live preview and guidelines: Logo, Button, SectionHead, Label, StatusTag, TextField, CourseCard, Progress, Icon, Terminal, and the Guideline page. Six **Colour** cards draw the colour system as the Brand Guideline does: palette, shares and rules, Hacker mode colours, ramps, contrast, and colour, motion and mobile.
