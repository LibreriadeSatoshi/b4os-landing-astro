# 04 Rules

*Working. What not to do. Every rule has a reason and a test.*

## The mark · construction rules

- Solid fill, one colour, from the supplied file.
- No outline, stroke, shadow, gradient or bevel.
- Full contrast or nothing, never on a mid-tone.
- Never filled with a photograph or a texture.
- Never rotated. The books stay on the diagonals.
- Never inside a circle, badge, chip or rounded tile.
- Never redrawn, traced or rebuilt from this page.
- Never at partial opacity over type.
- Never in any colour but Ink or Paper.

## Misuse · eight failures

Eight failures, all of them things that have happened to marks like this one. Every one is wrong.

| | Failure | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Stretched | The geometry is the mark. |
| 02 | Rotated | The books stay on the diagonals. |
| 03 | In a badge | A container makes it a sticker. |
| 04 | Cropped to a fragment | A single book or pair reads as a broken file. |
| 05 | On a mid-tone | Full contrast or nothing. |
| 06 | With a shadow | Flat vector only. |
| 07 | Faded behind type | Never at partial opacity over type. |
| 08 | Two colours at once | One colour, always. |

**Also never.** Never animate the books apart. Never use the mark as a bullet, a checkbox, a loading spinner or a progress meter. Never use it as a decorative border. Never place a second logo inside its clear space: a co-branded lockup is set with a full-height hairline between the two marks and equal clear space either side of it.

## Colour

- No gradient between two palette colours. No rainbow rules, arcs or ribbons. No colour-coded difficulty.
- No colour per tag. Tags are told apart by their words.
- No tints of a colour to make it "softer" and no opacity to separate series. Where a lighter surface is wanted, the answer is Paper or Logwood 50.
- Weld near 6%: past that, the palette turns loud.
- Never a FAIL pair from the contrast table in 06 for the use it failed.
- Hacker mode: no pure-hue glow, no coloured shadow, no border in an accent to "separate" a card, no third elevation level.

**Do** let a page be almost entirely Ink and Paper with one bar of Mauveine at the top. That is a correct, finished ISMOS page. Colour is how the brand is recognised, not how much design a page has had.

## Type

- All caps for a heading, a title or a sentence. Uppercase belongs to the Plex Mono label only.
- Underline for emphasis. Underline means link.
- Italic for a whole paragraph. Italic is for a title, a foreign word, or a student's own voice.
- Faux bold or faux italic. If the weight does not exist, do not use it.
- Letterspacing on lowercase text, at any size.
- Exclamation marks in interface copy. One per page of prose, at most, and only in a student's own words.
- Spectral on an ISMOS surface; a fifth face; Bricolage Grotesque anywhere except the wordmark; Literata and Plex Sans in the same paragraph; a system serif standing in for Literata in a printed piece.

## Devices and layout

- No device outside the six in 01.1: no rounded panel, left-border callout, coloured chip, arc, dotted or double rule, gradient.
- No rule weight other than 1, 2 and 6 px.
- No spacing value outside the nine. If a layout needs 20 px, it needs 16 or 24.
- No corner above 8 px. No pills, no round buttons, no circular badges.
- No shadow except `shadow-float`. Cards use a hairline.
- Type never breaks the grid. A caption, a label, a footer and a lockup sit on the text column's margin.

## Icons

- Inside a circle, badge, chip or coloured tile.
- Filled, at any size, for any reason, including to show a selected state. Selected state is weight and position, not fill.
- Below 20 px. Use a word instead.
- Emoji, anywhere: not in the interface, not in course material, not in a post, not in an email subject line.
- The mark as an icon in a set of icons. It is solid and it means ISMOS.
- An icon in an accent colour on a surface that has no colour of its own.
- Two colours, Iconoir's solid style, or any icon from another library mixed with Iconoir.

## Images

- No fabric imagery for now: surfaces use Ink, Paper and flat colour blocks.
- No person depicted anywhere in ISMOS imagery or icons. Two exceptions: **teacher photos**, on the course card, the course page and the teacher's own class page; people in student and teacher blog stories (treatment not yet specified).
- Teacher photo: a real photo of the teacher, shared with their written consent. Circle crop, full colour, head and shoulders on a plain background, no filter, frame, badge or overlay, 1 px `hairline` ring. Sizes: 32 px on a course card, 64 px on a course page, 96 px at the top of the teacher's own class page. Alt text is the teacher's name; empty when the name is printed beside it, so it is not read twice. No photo, or data saver on: the initials avatar. Students never have a photo.
- Never a teacher photo on the home page hero, slides, social images, print or the conference booth.
- No student is ever asked to upload a photograph; there is no surface where a student's own image is published.
- No regional pattern, traditional motif or caption naming a place, a people or a technique. Never *heritage*, *ancestral*, *tribal* or *authentic*.

## The test

**Every rule reduces to one property: a student can read it straight away, and someone with the files and a straight edge can check it.** One mark from the file, one colour per element, three rule weights, nine spacing values, the type scale, contrast from the table.

Ask instead of "does it look good?": *could a teacher rebuild this from memory on a borrowed laptop, and would a student read it first time on a cheap phone?*
