# 02 Principles

*Settled. Four rules. Everything in 03 to 08 follows from them.*

Picture your student. On a phone. Often at night. Maybe in a second language. One sentence to decide: is this place for me?

These rules protect that moment. They aren't taste. You can check each one.

## 1. Make the plainer version.

Your student should read the words straight away. No decoding a colour, a diagram or a layout first.

*Why:* the best teaching is understood on the first read. Ornament slows the reader down.

## 2. Place the artwork. Never redraw it.

Use the mark, the book motif, the icons and the hex values exactly as supplied.

*Why:* approximate one piece and the set stops being a set.

## 3. Never let colour carry meaning alone.

Every state gets a colour, an icon and a word. Tags are told apart by their words, not colour.

*Why:* about 1 in 12 men can't tell Madder from Weld. And a colour per tag is one more thing to learn.

## 4. Name the person, then the number.

Open with someone's outcome. Give every figure its denominator. Never make anyone feel behind.

*Why:* nobody is behind. They're not yet reached.

## The bar for every sentence

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
