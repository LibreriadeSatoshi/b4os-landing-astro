# 04.1 Do and do not

*Working. Pairs. Each bad example names how many rules it breaks.*

## The mark on a surface

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Ink mark on Paper, from the file, at ≥ 20 px, with 0.25 M clear space. | The mark rotated 22°, at 32% opacity, inside a rounded tile, behind the words *Cohort 04*. |

The bad example breaks **four** rules: rotated, faded behind type, in a badge, type in the clear space.

## A page

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Paper ground, Ink type, one 6 px Mauveine bar, the course's tags, a Literata title, Plex Sans body, facts in Plex Mono behind a hairline. | A Mauveine-to-Indigo gradient hero, a different colour for each tag, a rounded 16 px card with a shadow, a heading in all caps, an icon filled to show it is selected. |

The bad example breaks **five** rules: gradient, colour per tag, corner above 8 px plus a shadow, uppercase heading, filled icon. Each is a decision someone made to rescue the one before it.

## A status

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| A 2 px Madder 800 border, the Alert icon, and *Error: add a domain ending, e.g. ana@ismos.org*. | A Madder border alone, no icon, no message. |

Breaks **one** rule that hides three: colour alone carries the state.

By the end you'll have set up a wallet, backed it up, and recovered it once on purpose. | Ready to finally take control of your money? Let's go! In just 4 weeks you'll be a self-custody pro. |

## A story

**Do.** Kley left Venezuela for Ecuador, then Chile. Four years ago he was a bookkeeper. He took our first Lightning Network course, we took it together, actually, and became a node administrator for a LatAm exchange. Then he started coding. Today he's a full-time B4OS grantee, building the open-source tools that help the next Kley move faster than he did.

- *Person first:* his name and his life open the paragraph; the program is named fifth.
- *Specific work:* bookkeeper, node administrator, grantee. Real jobs, in order, no adjectives.
- *Our own imperfection:* "we took it together", the teacher was learning too.
- *Ends on the multiplier:* the next Kley.

**Do not.** ISMOS is proud to have transformed the life of a Venezuelan migrant through our world-class Bitcoin education programs, demonstrating the unparalleled impact of our mission-driven approach to closing the opportunity gap.

Breaks **four** rules: ISMOS is the subject; Kley is erased into a category; four banned words (transformed, world-class, unparalleled, impact) and nothing verifiable; nobody speaks like this.

## If in doubt

Make the plainer version: Ink on Paper, one bar, the words. Take the safer contrast pair. Set the type in Ink.
