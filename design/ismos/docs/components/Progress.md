# Progress

One learner's own advance through a course: a bar, and a sentence.

- **Provide:** the course name, the count as a figure (*5 of 8*), the fill width, and one plain sentence.
- 6 px `bar` fill on `track`. Count in Plex Mono, tabular.
- Never a percentage alone. Zero progress is an empty bar and a plain sentence, never a warning colour.
- No streaks, badges, points or confetti. Nothing tells a student they are behind.
