# Logo

The ISMOS mark (four books on the diagonals around an open centre), the lockup and the book motif. Consumers place the supplied SVG files; never redraw the mark.

- **Files:** `assets/Logos/ismos-mark-ink.svg` (Ink `#000000`), `ismos-mark-paper.svg` (Paper `#FFFFFF`), `ismos-book-ink.svg`, `ismos-book-paper.svg`. `ismos-mark-supplied.png` is the raster reference as supplied.
- **Provide:** the container and its ground. Ink mark on `paper`; Paper mark on `ink`, `logwood`, `indigo` or `mauveine`.
- **Lockup:** wordmark in `wordmark` (Bricolage Grotesque 500, 0.04em), address in `address` (Plex Mono 500, 0.40em). Proportions in *Identity*. The preview sets it from the fonts only to show the proportions; no supplied lockup file exists yet.
- **Sizes:** mark ≥ 20 px (7 mm), stacked lockup ≥ 96 px, horizontal ≥ 128 px. Clear space 0.25 M.
- **Don't:** rotate, outline, shadow, badge, tint, two colours, partial opacity over type, anything in the centre.
