# SectionHead

Opens a section in documents and long pages: number, title, 2 px rule, right kicker.

- **Provide:** the number (Plex Mono, `text-muted`), the title (Literata, `text-heading`), the kicker (`.ism-label`).
- The 2 px `rule-strong` closes the heading. No other rule weight.
- Title in sentence case; never all caps.
