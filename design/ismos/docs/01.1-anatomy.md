# 01.1 Anatomy

*Settled. The parts of the system, and which are required.*

## The mark

Four books open to four compass points, set on the diagonals around an open centre. It is drawn from embroidery patterns found across regions and it is the same mark the Foundation uses, at the same proportions, from the same file. Place `assets/Logos/ismos-mark-ink.svg` or `ismos-mark-paper.svg`.

The centre is empty on purpose. Nothing goes in it: no letter, no number, no cohort year, no initial. The four books are one object. A single book, or a single pair, is not a logo, it reads as a broken file.

## The lockup

Mark, wordmark, and the address underneath. The Foundation's descender line reads ISMOS.FOUNDATION; ISMOS's reads ISMOS.XYZ. Everything else about the lockup is identical, so the two sit side by side without argument.

1. **Mark** · solid, one colour, from the supplied file.
2. **Wordmark** · ISMOS, Bricolage Grotesque Medium, letterspaced 0.04 em (`wordmark`). Artwork, not type.
3. **Address line** · ISMOS.XYZ, IBM Plex Mono Medium, letterspaced 0.40 em (`address`).

- **Stacked** is the default. **Horizontal** for narrow spaces, headers, signatures.

| Proportion (M = width of the mark) | Value |
|---|---|
| Wordmark cap height | 0.36 M |
| Mark to wordmark, stacked | 0.28 M |
| Mark to wordmark, horizontal | 0.30 M |
| Wordmark to address line | 0.14 M |
| Address line, letterspacing | 0.40 em |
| Address line width, stacked | Set to the width of the wordmark, optically |

- **Do** place the supplied lockup file. Use the mark alone where the address is already on the page: inside the platform, on a favicon, as an avatar, at the top of a slide that has a footer.
- **Don't** typeset the wordmark from a font. Don't add a course or tag to the descender line. Don't set the address line in Literata, in sentence case, or with www or https in front of it.

## Clear space and minimum size

Clear space is 0.25 M on all four sides, measured from the outermost corners of the books. Nothing enters it: no type, no rule, no image edge, no other logo. On the platform, the header's own padding usually satisfies it already.

| | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Mark alone | 20 px | 7 mm |
| Stacked lockup | 96 px | 24 mm |
| Horizontal lockup | 128 px | 32 mm |
| Favicon, app icon | 16 px | — |

Below 20 px the books close up and the open centre fills in. At 16 px, use the supplied favicon artwork, which is drawn for that size; do not scale the standard mark down to it. At size: 32 mm and 16 mm hold; 7 mm is the minimum; 4 mm is too small.

## Where each version belongs

- **Ink on Paper.** The default. Home page, anything covering more than one topic, documents, records of completion, co-branding.
- **Paper on a colour block** (Mauveine, Indigo, Logwood). Full-bleed colour surfaces, slide openers, social frames.
- **Paper on Ink.** Hacker mode, signage, the app icon, avatars, and any surface shared with the Foundation.

The mark is Ink or Paper only.

## The book motif

Four books mean the institution. One book means a course. This is the only fragment of the mark that is ever permitted, and it exists because a course card should not make the same claim as the whole institution. Place `assets/Logos/ismos-book-ink.svg` or `ismos-book-paper.svg`: the upper-left book, both pages, unaltered, in its own square artwork cropped to the book's bounds. Never redrawn, never rotated to face another way, never mirrored, never separated into single pages.

| What each one claims | |
|---|---|
| Four books, Ink or Paper | ISMOS.XYZ, or ISMOS.FOUNDATION |
| One book, Ink or Paper | A course |

- **Where it goes:** course cards (one per card, top left), lesson headers beside the course name, course listings and search results, a record of completion beside the course named. Minimum 16 px; below that, use the course name alone.
- **Never:** as a logo, an avatar, a favicon or an app icon; in the navigation, where the full lockup belongs; in the same block as the full mark; inside a circle, badge, chip or rounded container; tiled or repeated as a pattern.

## The motif field

Tiling the mark is permitted in exactly one situation: as a flat field of repeats behind nothing, used as a ground for a cover, an empty state, or the back of a printed card. One colour on one colour, even spacing, no rotation, no varied sizes, no partial marks at the edges except where the field is cropped by the surface: a cut book at the boundary is the tile working, not a fragment. Maximum opacity 25% against its ground. Never place body text on the field; a title or the lockup may sit on it if the contrast rules in 06 still pass.

## Tags

ISMOS organises its courses with **tags**: words that say what a course is about. A course carries more than one: *Local AI* is tagged **AI · Privacy · Freedom Tech**. Show at most three on a course card. The set of tags is open; add one when a course needs it.

- Set as the Plex Mono uppercase `label` in `text-muted` (Logwood 800 on Paper, Logwood 200 in Hacker mode), or Paper on a colour block, separated by a middle dot.
- No colour, chip, box or icon per tag. Every tag looks the same; the word tells them apart.
- Tags are for finding and grouping courses. They are not ranks, levels or badges.

## Graphic devices: six, and nothing else

Six devices carry every layout. The list is closed: anything not here, a rounded panel, a left-border callout, a coloured chip, an arc, a dotted rule, a gradient, is outside the system.

1. **Three rule weights** · 1 · 2 · 6 px. Hairline separates rows. The 2 px Ink rule closes a heading. The 6 px `bar` (Mauveine) opens a section or tops a card.
2. **The mark as a marker** · whole, small: section marker, avatar, favicon. Never a fragment, never rotated, never one book.
3. **Corner mark** · ≤ 24 px, top left of a page or card, on the text column's own margin. Not a watermark; never centred.
4. **Colour blocks** · flat, hard-edged, full bleed: a quarter of the surface or the whole of it. Nothing in between, and never a floating panel inset from the edges.
5. **Dashed construction line** · clear space, grid, measurement. Construction only, inside a document like this one. Never on a student-facing surface, never printed, never a decorative border.
6. **The motif field** · ≤ 25% opacity, one colour on one colour, cropped by the surface. No body text on it.

**Why so few.** Most of the material carrying this brand will be made by teachers and ambassadors in whatever tool they have, and by AI tools working from these pages. Six devices are few enough to remember without looking them up, and to rebuild from memory in a slide deck on a borrowed laptop.

## Required and optional

- **Required on every surface:** the mark or the address; Ink and Paper as the ground and the type; Literata or Plex Sans for the words.
- **Required on course surfaces:** the course's tags.
- **Optional:** the 6 px bar, a colour block, the motif field, the book motif on a course.
